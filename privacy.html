Privacy Policy

My Video Uploader ("the App") helps creators publish their own edited videos to their own TikTok accounts.

Data we collect

  • TikTok account information (username, open ID) — obtained only after you explicitly authorize the App via TikTok's OAuth login.
  • Access tokens — stored securely and used only to upload videos you approve to your own account.
  • Video files you choose to upload — processed temporarily for upload and not retained afterwards.

How we use data

Your data is used solely to publish videos to the TikTok account you authorized. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing.

Data retention

Access tokens are kept only while your authorization is active. You may revoke access at any time from your TikTok settings; we then delete stored tokens.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app owner through TikTok direct message on the connected account.
