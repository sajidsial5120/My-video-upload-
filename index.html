My Video Upload

A personal tool that uploads your own edited videos to your own TikTok account via TikTok's official Content Posting API.

How it works

  1. You connect your TikTok account once through TikTok's secure login.
  2. The tool uploads a video file you choose, with your caption.
  3. The video is published directly to your TikTok profile.
